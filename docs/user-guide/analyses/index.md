# The analyses

Eight analyses are applied to a phantom series. Seven of them store their
results in the [analysis file](../analysis-file.md). The eighth operates on a
pair of scans and stores nothing.

Each page states the purpose of the analysis and its method, shows a figure
generated by the pipeline from a synthetic phantom when the documentation is
built, and tabulates the quantities the analysis adds to the analysis file under
their netCDF names.

| Analysis | Quantity measured | Measurements stored |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [FBIRN stability](stability.md) | signal relative to static and temporal noise, the drift removed, and the departure of the noise from voxel-to-voxel independence | `snr`, `sfnr`, `rdc`, `drift_pct`, `percent_fluctuation` |
| [Phantom localization](localization.md) | the positions of the phantom and the summary ROI, and whether each was placed correctly | `phantom_fraction`, `phantom_contrast`, `phantom_mask_ok`, `roi_inside_phantom` |
| [Nyquist ghosting](ghosting.md) | the N/2 ghost relative to the signal that produced it, by four measures, and the axis on which it was measured | `ghost_pct`, `ghost_top_pct`, `ro_ghost_pct`, `acr_ghost_pct`, `pe_axis`, `pe_label` |
| [ADC saturation](saturation.md) | whether the receiver clipped, before and after the trim boundary, with a third outcome when this cannot be determined | `clipped_any`, `clipped_kept`, `n_clipped_kept`, `n_clipped_pre`, `clip_ceiling`, `clip_bits` |
| [Outliers and spikes](outliers.md) | the corrupted volume and the dominant periodic artifact that averages conceal | `outlier_max_pct`, `outlier_worst_frame`, `tc_outlier`, `spec_outlier` |
| [Motion and deformation](motion.md) | the translation, scale and shear that gradient heating produces, with a mask-free centroid trace for comparison | `max_translation`, `max_scale_pct`, `max_shear_pct`, `max_com_displacement`, `displacement_unit` |
| [Smoothness and point spread](smoothness.md) | the correlation length of the noise field in each direction, and the shape of the blur | `fwhm`, `fwhm_pe`, `fwhm_ro`, `fwhm_unit` |
| [Thermal versus instability noise](hilo.md) | the additive and multiplicative noise components, separated with a flip-angle pair | none (standard output only) |

The last column lists `measurements.*` attributes. The table on each page gives
their meanings, together with the variables, masks and coordinates that the
analysis owns.
